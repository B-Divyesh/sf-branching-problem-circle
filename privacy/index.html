Plain-language privacy

Privacy for circle data

Problems, approaches, votes, and rationales are saved in your browser’s local database. We do not receive them, create child accounts, or track participants.

What is stored

The app stores the circle you author, anonymous vote totals, written rationales, and reveal state in your browser.

What leaves the device

Nothing from a circle is sent to us. This version has no checkout or license verification request.

Your control

Use “Export data” to keep a JSON copy. “Clear circle” removes circle data from this browser. Removing site data in browser settings clears everything.

Contact

For privacy questions, email privacy@sociobot.in.
