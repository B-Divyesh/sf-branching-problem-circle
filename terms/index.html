Terms for using a math circle

Branching Problem Circle is a facilitation tool, not a curriculum, assessment, or source of generated solutions.

Your responsibility

You are responsible for the problems and notes you enter, including having permission to use them and deciding what is appropriate for your group. Do not enter identifying or sensitive information about children.

Included templates

Starter templates are included. Authoring, voting, reveals, printing, and export do not require a purchase.

Availability

The app is provided as-is without guarantees that it will fit every teaching setting. Local and offline behavior depends on your browser retaining site data. Keep JSON exports for important sessions.
